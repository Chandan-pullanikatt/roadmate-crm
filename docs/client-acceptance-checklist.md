# RoadMate CRM Client Acceptance Checklist

## Founder
- Login works and founder lands on the founder dashboard.
- State managers, industry managers, and executives can be created from the UI.
- Lead creation, CSV bulk upload, reallocation, leave approvals, salary incentive edits, and report exports work.

## State Manager
- Industry managers can be created.
- Team performance, attendance, leave, and lead management views load with real data.
- Escalated leads and mapped leads are visible and actionable.

## Industry Manager
- Executives can be created with district assignment.
- Staff documents can be uploaded and reopened.
- Leave approvals, lead mapping, and performance views work.

## Executive
- Start work creates the work session.
- Lead wizard completes a full cycle: call, outcome, feedback, follow-up, next lead.
- RNR updates reschedule correctly.
- Meeting scheduling, leave request, attendance completion, and performance summary work.

## Cross-Role
- Notifications are received for meeting scheduling and leave approval updates.
- Dashboard counts match underlying tables.
- Role-based access blocks unauthorized pages.
